Vishwas Dhabhai

I am a Data Science M.S. candidate at the University of Pennsylvania (Expected Spring 2028), mentored by Prof. Boon Thau Loo. My research interests are broadly in distributed systems for large-scale machine learning workloads — how post-training, model serving, and retrieval get built and scaled in practice. I'm particularly drawn to the systems side of agentic and RL-based workflows, where training, rollouts, and evaluation all have to run reliably at scale, and I'd like to understand that infrastructure better.

I am seeking research collaborations, as well as full-time pre-doctoral research roles in industry starting in 2026.

At Santander Corporate & Investment Banking, I worked on distributed data infrastructure for equity derivatives trading building data pipelines with parallel, partition-based execution across clusters to process millions of high-frequency market records daily, sustaining the low latency real-time trading desks need.

Before academia, I spent ~1.5 years in Data and AI engineering — building LLM-powered automation systems at Incedo and machine learning models as a co-op at IBM.
