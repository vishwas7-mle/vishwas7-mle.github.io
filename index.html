Vishwas Dhabhai

I am a Data Science M.S. candidate at the University of Pennsylvania (Expected Spring 2028), mentored by Prof. Boon Thau Loo. My research interests center on the infrastructure that spans the full lifecycle of building and improving large models and agents — from selecting what data they train on, to how they're trained and served, to how agentic systems evaluate and improve themselves over time. I'm particularly drawn to the systems side of this lifecycle: making data curation, post-training, serving, and agentic self-improvement loops run efficiently and reliably at scale.

I am seeking research collaborations, as well as full-time pre-doctoral research roles in industry starting in 2026.

At Santander Corporate & Investment Banking, I worked on distributed data infrastructure for equity derivatives trading building data pipelines with parallel, partition-based execution across clusters to process millions of high-frequency market records daily, sustaining the low latency real-time trading desks need.

Previously, I spent ~1.5 years in Data and AI engineering — building LLM-powered automation systems at Incedo and machine learning models as a co-op at IBM.
