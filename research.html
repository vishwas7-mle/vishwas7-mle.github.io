Research

My interests sit at the intersection of machine learning and systems:

Data curation and selection for LLM training

Choosing the right training and fine-tuning data for a target task — data representations, selection algorithms, and the trade-offs between them.

Distributed systems for LLM workloads

Efficient, reliable infrastructure for post-training pipelines, multi-stage workloads, and large-scale model development.

LLM serving and retrieval

High-throughput, low-latency model serving, retrieval systems, scheduling, caching, and resource-aware execution.

Agentic systems and self-improvement

Systems that make training, rollouts, evaluation, and iteration for agentic learning more efficient and dependable — including infrastructure for pipelines and agents that evaluate and improve themselves over time.
